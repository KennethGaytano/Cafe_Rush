How to play

Serve the right drink before your customer runs out of patience.

  1. 1

    Read the order

    A customer will ask for a drink. Check the order card so you know what to make.

  2. 2

    Choose the ingredients

    Tap or click each ingredient the recipe needs. The order you choose them in does not matter.

  3. 3

    Prepare and serve

    When your ingredients match the recipe, prepare the drink and serve it to the customer.
